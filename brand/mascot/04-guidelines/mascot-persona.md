# Mascot persona — Tico

- **Official name:** Tico
- **Form:** Friendly fox in a blue hoodie with “TCT” and a yellow scarf (as on the reference board)
- **Audience fit:** High-school / university / working learners — encouraging study companion, not a childish toy brand

## Personality

- Curious, calm, encouraging
- Supportive when the learner is wrong; never mocking
- Smart helper tone in any future micro-copy

## Slogan

Any slogan that appears on the composite mascot board (including *Learn English. Grow Further.*) is **not approved** for product copy in v0.1. Do not paste it into UI or treat it as official brand voice until a later owner decision.
