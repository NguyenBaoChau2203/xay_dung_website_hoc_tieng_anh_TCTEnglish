# Tico color and do/don’t

## Colors observed on the board

Aligned with the logo board sampling where shared (hoodie blue, scarf yellow, dark accents). Exact HEX may differ slightly due to JPG compression; prefer the cropped PNG/SVG assets over retyping colors by eye.

## Do

- Keep hoodie + scarf + “TCT” chest mark consistent across poses
- Use the pose that matches the UX state in `expression-map.md`
- Prefer transparent PNG/SVG from this kit

## Do not

- Redraw Tico in a new art style
- Use Tico as the website favicon or header logo
- Show a mocking or aggressive expression for wrong answers
- Attach unapproved slogan text beside the mascot in product UI (v0.1)
