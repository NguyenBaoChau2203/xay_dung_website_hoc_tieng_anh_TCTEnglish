# Advanced editorial review — 2026-11-14

The current agent authored and reviewed all 36 lessons, 432 Vietnamese–English pairs and 36 selective alternatives inline. Review covered subject and reference, tense and time, negation, quantities, modal strength, register, natural wording and paragraph continuity. This is editorial QA by the authoring agent; it is not an independent teacher review or CEFR certification.

Each lesson has 12 ordered pairs and three paragraphs, with breaks after sentences 4, 8 and 12. Titles, topics and previews describe the actual communicative purpose. The advanced level uses qualified claims, subordinate clauses, ambiguity, trade-offs and professional or reflective register, rather than adding filler to an intermediate text. There is one deliberately selected semantic alternative per lesson; the lists are not exhaustive translation dictionaries.

Emails address recipients and define requests, boundaries or next steps. Diaries show an experience followed by reflection and an unresolved nuance or practical change. Essays state a position, consider a counterargument and qualify the conclusion. Articles address readers with explanatory guidance. Stories have an initiating event, discovery or conflict and a resolution. Reports separate their purpose and method, findings and limitations, then recommendations. All six reports explicitly identify the scenario and numbers as fictional in both languages.

The agent checked each alternative against its Vietnamese sentence, preserving negation, timing, scope and obligation. No exact-match assessment result is treated as proof of semantic fidelity. The unchanged local assessment policy accepts all references and approved alternatives and rejects empty and unrelated submissions.
